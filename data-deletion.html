Data Deletion Instructions

Application: Explorer TV MasterBlaster

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Request deletion of your data

Users who want to request deletion of personal information associated with Explorer TV MasterBlaster may email:

Email: contact.explorertv@gmail.com

Use the subject line Data Deletion Request. If your request concerns Facebook or another Meta service, use Meta Data Deletion Request.

What to include

Tell us which account or sign-in provider your request concerns. Do not send your password or other secret authentication information. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership.

What happens next

After receiving and verifying a request, we will review the information associated with the account, explain any applicable retention requirements, and delete eligible information when the request is processed. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

This page explains how to submit a request. It does not automatically delete data when the page is opened.

Contact

For questions about personal information or deletion requests, contact contact.explorertv@gmail.com.
